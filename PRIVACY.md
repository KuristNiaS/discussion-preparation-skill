# Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Discussion Preparation is an instruction-only, skills-only plugin. It does not operate an external server, create user accounts, use cookies, or independently collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

The plugin processes only the prompts and files that a user chooses to provide within Codex or ChatGPT. Those materials are handled by the OpenAI product through which the plugin is used and remain subject to that product's applicable privacy terms and workspace settings. The plugin author does not receive those prompts, files, translations, or discussion notes through this plugin.

The public source repository is hosted by GitHub. Visiting or interacting with that repository is subject to GitHub's own privacy practices.

For privacy questions, open an issue at https://github.com/KuristNiaS/discussion-preparation-skill/issues.
